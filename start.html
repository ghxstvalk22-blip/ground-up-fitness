Goal 01

Lose weight

Fat loss comes from eating a little less than you burn. Lifting makes sure what you lose is fat, not the muscle that keeps you strong.

  1. Estimate your daily calories and protein.
  2. Lift 3 to 4 days a week.
  3. Walk every day, even on rest days.
  4. Weigh in a few mornings a week and watch the trend, not the single day.

Goal 02

Get stronger

Strength is a skill you practice. A few big lifts, done often, with a little more weight over time, is how almost everyone gets strong.

  1. Learn the squat, bench, deadlift, and overhead press.
  2. Start lighter than you think you need to.
  3. Add a small amount of weight each week.
  4. Write down every session so you know what to beat.

Goal 03

I'm brand new

Nobody at the gym is watching you as closely as you think. Your only job for the first month is to show up and learn the movements.

  1. Week 1: go 3 times. Use light weights and learn each exercise.
  2. Week 2: same plan, add a little weight where it felt easy.
  3. Pick the same days and times each week so it becomes routine.
  4. Read the gym basics below before your first session.
