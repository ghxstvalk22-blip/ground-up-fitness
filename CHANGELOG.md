# Changelog

Newest changes first. Each daily update adds an entry here.

## 2026-10-15 · Things stay open while you work out

- **Open things stay open.** Tapping + set, checking off an exercise, swapping or logging used to redraw the day and snap every open section shut. Now the day remembers: an open **More**, an open set log and the **···** menu all stay open. Saving your sets closes just that log, as you'd expect.
- **"More" is a regular button** at the end of the Log sets row. It no longer sits over the Log sets button, which could make taps on iPhone hit the wrong thing.
- The "Use Ground Up like an app" tip no longer covers the rest timer.

## 2026-10-15 · A calmer workout screen

- **Each exercise shows only what you need mid-workout:** its number, name, sets × reps · weight, the muscle it works, **Log sets**, and **Rest**. A small "↑ Heavier than last time" shows when the weight goes up. Everything else (− set / + set, the easier option, last time's numbers and tips, How to, Swap) is one tap away under **More**. Each exercise now takes about half the space.
- **Day options in one place:** Edit, Share, move up/down and Remove day are behind a single **···** button at the top of each day, and "What this workout hits" is a quiet link.

## 2026-10-14 · Workouts from friends folds away

- "Workouts from friends" on the Plans page is now one slim line instead of a big block. Tap it to open **Paste workout** and the paste box, and tap again to close it.

## 2026-10-14 · What this workout hits

- **New button on every day: "What this workout hits."** Tap it any time to see the body map for that day. Orange shows the muscles it works, and stripes show muscles worked by 2 or more exercises. Below that, each muscle is listed with the exercises that hit it, plus what it works a little. It's the same view as Build your own workout, there whenever you want a reminder. It works on ready-made plans too.
- **Fix: "− set" works on Your plan.** Your goal and time settings were changing the number of sets on workouts you built yourself, so a day could show 5 sets while "− set" was greyed out. On your own plan, the sets you pick now always win: what you see is what you saved, and "− set" goes down to 1. Ready-made plans still adjust to your goal.
- **Fix:** some buttons (like "Put it in the best order") showed the phone's plain blue default look. They now use the site's black-and-white style.

## 2026-10-14 · Your plan puts itself in the right order

- **Every workout you build is saved in the order to do it.** Just go top to bottom; the exercises are numbered 1, 2, 3. The order follows standard training practice:
  - big lifts first, while you're fresh (squats, presses, rows)
  - then other multi-muscle moves
  - then single-muscle moves (leg extension, curls, raises)
  - core and cardio last
  - no muscle three times in a row
- **Adding an exercise** (from the library, the Muscle map, or a swap) puts it in its right place, not just at the end.
- **Plans you already made** show "There's a better order for this day" with a one-tap **Put it in the best order**. Your check-marks stay with their exercises.
- **Each exercise shows the muscle it works** (Quads, Glutes, Chest…).
- **Too much of one muscle in a day** (like 5 quad exercises) gets a note and a one-tap swap to a muscle the day is missing, like hamstrings or glutes.

## 2026-10-14 · The live dot blinks for everyone

- The Your plan bar on other pages has the same live dot, so it's clear your plan is running.

## 2026-10-14 · Checked against real lifters: realistic weight jumps

We ran six simulated lifters (new man and woman, 2-year intermediate, advanced and elite bodybuilders, and a strength lifter with heavy and light days) through 18 weeks of training. Each had a real strength level that grew at a realistic pace, and the app picked every weight. The app never made a jump a real person couldn't: the biggest single step was one dumbbell size (+5 lb), +5 lb on upper-body barbell lifts, and +10–20 lb on squats, deadlifts and leg press (under 10%). Fixed what it found:

- **Easing off after two tough sessions lands on a real weight.** 325 goes to 290, not 292.5, and dumbbells drop one real size.
- **No more getting stuck on light dumbbells.** 10% off a 10 lb dumbbell used to round back to 10. Now it always drops at least one size.
- **The first time you do a lift in a new rep range** (like a light day after a heavy one), the suggested weight leaves 2 reps in the tank and rounds down to a loadable weight.

## 2026-10-14 · The day you're on comes first

- **"Your active plan" now has a live dot** that pulses, so it's clear which plan is running. (It stays still for people who turn on Reduce Motion.)
- **Fix:** with the "Get stronger" goal, a bodyweight first exercise (like a glute bridge) no longer gets heavy-lifting sets of 3–5 reps. That rule is now only for lifts with weight.

## 2026-10-14 · Paste workout: a friend's workout becomes yours in one tap

- **New "Workouts from friends" section on Plans.** Copy the link a friend sent, open the app, and tap **Paste workout** (allow the paste when your phone asks). It becomes your active workout right away, as the day you're on: on Day 3, it's your Day 3, and your old Day 3 and the days after it move down one. Plans opens on it with a note saying who it's from.
- **Why paste?** iPhone always opens links in Safari, never in Home Screen web apps, and the Home Screen app keeps its own saved data. When a shared link opens in Safari on an iPhone, it now starts with **Copy for the app** and the 3 steps.
- If the phone won't allow the paste, there's a box to paste the link into instead.
- **Android:** links to the site open in the installed app when the phone supports it.

## 2026-10-14 · Share a workout with a friend

- **Share button on every day of Your plan.** Tap Share, pick that day or all your days, and send it any way you like (Messages, AirDrop, WhatsApp). On a computer, the link is copied for you.
- **It slots in where your friend is.** When they open it, they see who sent it and the exercises. One tap makes it the day they're on: if they're on Day 3, it becomes their Day 3 and their old Day 3 (and the days after it) move down one. They can also add it after their last day or start fresh with it.
- **Private by design.** The workout travels inside the link itself, not through a server. Only exercises, sets, reps and rest are shared, never your weights, logs or settings. Your friend's app works out weights for them. Your name is included only if you leave "Say it's from…" ticked.
- **Using the Home Screen app?** It keeps its own saved data, separate from Safari. So Plans now has an "Add a workout a friend sent" button to paste the link into.

## 2026-10-14 · Rest alarms: a buzz when rest is over, no app switching

- **No more jump to Shortcuts.** Starting a rest no longer opens the Shortcuts app on its own. The "Lock Screen timer" button is still there as a backup, but only where alarms can't be used.
- **New: Buzz me when rest is over.** Tap it once (and allow notifications). After that, every rest buzzes your phone at the exact second it's over, even with the phone locked or in another app. The timer shows "alarm set ✓". On iPhone this works in the Home Screen app.
- **How it works:** iPhone pauses web apps in the background, so the site's own small server (the same Cloudflare Worker as the Fitness Coach) sends the buzz. It only gets an anonymous notification address for your phone and the time your rest ends, deletes them when the alarm goes off, and never sees your workouts. It's off until you turn it on, and the privacy answer on Gym basics now says so.
- **Owner setup:** the Worker now deploys itself from GitHub. Add two secrets once (steps in `coach-ai/README.md`). Until then the app works exactly as before.

Testers had seen "1 workout this week" right next to "2 workout days, 20 sets" because half-finished sessions were counted in some places and not others.

## 2026-10-14 · Height, exact age, and body fat for precise numbers

- **Your starting point has a new "Your body" section** under Tailor it to you: exact age, height (feet and inches, or cm), body fat % if you know it, and how active you are outside the gym. All optional, saved only on your device.
- **Right there, it shows your daily calories and protein.** Calories use weight, height, age and sex (the Mifflin-St Jeor formula). If you add body fat %, it switches to the lean-mass formula (Katch-McArdle), which is more precise for very lean or muscular people. With higher body fat, protein is based on a healthy target weight so it isn't overstated.
- **The calorie tool fills itself in** from your profile, has a body fat field too, and saves what you change back to your profile. It now says which formula it used and where to get a personal plan (a registered dietitian or your doctor). The 1,500/1,200 calorie floors are unchanged.
- **Starting weights use your exact age** (a little lighter from 40, more from 50 and 60). Height isn't used for weights: strength standards go by bodyweight, sex, age and training level, and the lifts you enter beat any formula.

## 2026-10-14 · Strength targets, starting weights, kg, and plan picks

- **Heavy and light days of the same lift keep their own targets.** In Next-level strength, squat is heavy (5 × 3) one day and lighter (3 × 10) another. Each now gets its next weight from the same kind of day, so the heavy target no longer comes from the light day. The first time you do a lift in a new rep range, the weight is worked out from your other sets.
- **Starting weights fit the number of sets.** About 2% lighter for each set past two, so 5 sets of 5 starts lighter than 2 sets of 5, and 4 sets of 10 deadlifts no longer start too heavy.
- **Kilograms:** barbell, machine and cable weights now round to 2.5 kg steps (most gyms can't load 1.25 kg). Dumbbells keep their small steps.
- **Strong lifters get the right plan.** If the lifts you enter (with your bodyweight) are at Intermediate or Advanced strength, Best fit takes that into account. A note offers "Set my level to Advanced" (or whichever fits) in one tap.
- **Training at home?** Your starting weights now show dumbbell moves (goblet squat, floor press, row, Romanian deadlift), each hand, and skip moves you said to avoid.

## 2026-10-14 · Second round of real-world testing

Four testers worked through the whole app on a phone as different gym-goers (a nervous beginner, a lifter building his own split, a 58-year-old training at home with dumbbells, and an advanced strength lifter). What changed from what they found:

- **Smarter weight increases.** Each logged workout now remembers the sets and reps the plan asked for that day. A Heavy week (fewer reps) no longer looks like "missed sets", and a Recover or light week no longer becomes your new starting weight. Jumps are smaller on heavy lifts (squat 300 → 320 at most, not 330), and dumbbell lifts go up to the next dumbbell (20 → 25, not 30).
- **Switching pounds and kilograms converts your numbers** (195 lb becomes 88.5 kg) instead of just changing the label.
- **Fitness Coach:** "How much should I eat to lose weight?" now gets the food answer (calorie tool and a registered dietitian), not lifting advice. Home workouts skip moves that need a bench, and every Coach workout skips the moves you said to avoid.
- **Home with dumbbells** never swaps in a bench or barbell move.
- **Rest days are respected.** On ready-made plans, Progress no longer suggests a different workout the day after training; it says to rest a day. After finishing, "do the next day now" is a small link instead of a big button, and "Done for today" takes you back to the workout you finished.
- **The Active tab, plan bar and Up next show this week's real sets and reps** (the same as the Plans page).
- **The finish summary gives the same "next time" advice as the plan.** A walk you checked off now counts toward your weekly cardio minutes.
- **Safer:**
  - Cancel workout asks before clearing sets you typed but didn't save.
  - Finishing a day with nothing checked or logged asks first (it was easy to finish the wrong day).
- **Easier to read:**
  - The Progress tabs are bigger (two rows of three on phones).
  - "Your goal" shows once per day instead of on every exercise.
  - "Empty bar (45 lb)" now says the weight.
  - Your own plan no longer says "Beginner".
  - The calorie tool's menus aren't cut off.
  - There's space at the bottom of each page so the Coach button doesn't cover buttons.
- **iPhone rest timer:** the "why" note shows only the first few times, and the setup has an Open Shortcuts button.

The bigger items they found (separate targets for heavy and light versions of the same lift, lighter starting weights for more sets, and a few Progress tweaks) are on the roadmap.

## 2026-10-13 (Smarter weight jumps, research-based sets and reps, and ± sets on your plan)

- **Next time's weight is smarter.** The app looks at every set you did last time:
  - Hit the top of your rep range on every set: the weight goes up about 2.5% on upper-body lifts and about 5% on legs (at least 5 lb and 10 lb; light lifts like lateral raises go up in the smallest step, 2.5 lb).
  - Beat the top of the range by 2 or more reps on every set: a bigger jump (about 5% upper body, 10% legs), still within the 2 to 10% the research recommends.
  - Did fewer sets than planned (say 1 of 3): the weight stays the same until you get all the sets in.
- **Sets and reps follow your goal, based on the research** (the National Strength and Conditioning Association's guidelines and the American College of Sports Medicine's position stand): strength is 1 to 6 reps for 2 to 6 sets with 2 to 5 minutes of rest (main lifts: 4 × 3–5, or 3 × 5 if you're newer); muscle is 6 to 12 reps for 3 to 6 sets with 1 to 2 minutes of rest; endurance is 12 or more reps for 2 to 3 sets with short rests. This applies to Build your own workout, the Fitness Coach, and the main lift of a ready-made plan when your goal (Tailor it to you) is Get stronger. "Why these limits?" in the builder explains it with sources.
- **± sets right on your plan.** On Your plan, each exercise now has **− set** and **+ set** buttons, so you can add or take away a set without rebuilding the workout.
