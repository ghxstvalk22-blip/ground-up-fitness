Train like a pro

The habits Olympia champions and record holders share, scaled down for your first year in the gym.

  1. 01

    Progressive overload

    Every champion physique was built one small jump at a time: a little more weight, or one more rep, than last time.

    Start with: write down every set
  2. 02

    Consistency for years

    Pro physiques take many years of training. The plan you stick with beats the perfect plan you quit in March.

    Start with: three fixed days a week
  3. 03

    Hard sets, clean reps

    The pros push their working sets close to failure, but they control every rep. Sloppy reps build injuries, not muscle.

    Start with: how heavy to go
  4. 04

    Eat for the goal

    Protein every day, and calories matched to what you're after: a little less to lose fat, a little more to build muscle.

    Start with: your calorie and protein targets
  5. 05

    Recovery is training

    Muscle grows between workouts, not during them. Seven to nine hours of sleep and real rest days are part of the program.

    Start with: training when you're sore
  6. 06

    Master the basics

    Strip away the highlight reels and pro programs still center on squats, presses, rows, and hinges, done well, for years.

    Start with: your first two weeks

A reality check: top professional physiques come from many years of work, genetics, and in many cases performance-enhancing drugs. Use them for inspiration and ideas, and compare yourself only to last month's you.
